🏆 How to Play Ludo

• Rolling 6: Unlocks a token from base to start track, or gives an extra roll.


• Capturing: Land on an opponent's token to send them back to base and earn a bonus roll!


• Safe Stars: Tokens on Star tiles or colored start squares cannot be captured.


• Home Goal: Travel around 52 track cells and up colored home corridor. First to get all 4 tokens home wins!
